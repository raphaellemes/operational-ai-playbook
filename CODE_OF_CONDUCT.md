# Code Of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to keep this project respectful, practical, and useful.

Contributors are expected to participate with professionalism, curiosity, and good faith.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful.
- Assume positive intent.
- Debate ideas, not people.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Keep examples safe and non-confidential.
- Credit sources when relevant.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Publishing confidential, private, or personal data.
- Sharing credentials, secrets, or proprietary materials.
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to the project.
- Disruptive or hostile conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or block participants who violate these expectations.
