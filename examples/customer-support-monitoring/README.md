# Example: Customer Support Monitoring

This fictional example shows how an AI workflow can support quality monitoring in customer support.

## Process

Support interactions are reviewed to identify:

- dissatisfaction signals;
- unresolved requests;
- policy violations;
- escalation opportunities;
- coaching points.

## AI Role

The AI system may:

- summarize the interaction;
- classify the risk level;
- identify evidence in the transcript;
- recommend review priority.

The AI system may not:

- penalize an agent automatically;
- contact the customer;
- update HR records;
- close a compliance issue without human validation.

## Criticality

Medium.

Reason: AI influences prioritization and quality review, but a human supervisor validates before any operational action.

## Suggested Controls

- Transcript source identification;
- classification reason;
- evidence snippets;
- reviewer approval;
- decision log retained for 180 days.
