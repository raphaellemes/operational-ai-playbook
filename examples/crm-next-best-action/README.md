# Example: CRM Next Best Action

This fictional example shows an AI workflow that recommends the next best action in a CRM operation.

## Process

The commercial or customer success team needs to decide what to do next with an account.

Possible recommendations:

- follow-up;
- renewal review;
- satisfaction check;
- escalation;
- no action.

## AI Role

The AI system recommends an action based on:

- recent interactions;
- account status;
- SLA or contract information;
- open issues;
- business rules.

## Boundaries

Allowed:

- summarize account context;
- recommend next step;
- draft message;
- explain rationale.

Escalated:

- send message to client;
- change account status;
- open escalation.

Denied:

- change pricing;
- approve discounts;
- override contract rules.

## Criticality

Medium.

Reason: the recommendation can influence customer relationship, but execution should remain human-approved.
